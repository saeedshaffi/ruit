# Research plan

Two things in this review are judgment calls that deserve evidence before build.

## Questions

1. Do sellers who already list elsewhere choose Import first, and does leading with it reduce manual re-entry?
2. Can sellers add five similar products with the grid faster and with fewer errors than with five passes of the form?
3. Do sellers understand the difference between linking an account and publishing to it?
4. Can sellers tell two accounts on the same marketplace apart in the Products table?

## Method

Moderated usability test on the prototype's explore mode. 5 sellers per round, 2 rounds, 30 minutes each. Mix: 3 who sell on two or more marketplaces, 2 who have more than one account on a single marketplace.

## Tasks

1. "You already sell 20 items on eBay. Get them into Ruit." Observe: first click, time to first import.
2. "You have five new jackets, all in good condition, all shipped. Add them." Observe: use of shared values, errors at review, time.
3. "Make the Nike trainers available on both of your eBay accounts, but do not put them on sale yet." Observe: route taken, whether Publish is tried, understanding of the confirmation.
4. "Which accounts is the Levi's jacket on, and is anything wrong with it?" Observe: reads the row or opens the popover, finds the attention state.

## Measures

Task success, first click, time on task, errors, and a Single Ease Question after each task.

## Also check in analytics

Share of new products created by import versus manual entry, and how many products are created per manual session. If most manual sessions add one product, keep "One product" as the default tab. If import is rarely used by accounts with live listings, the entry point hierarchy is the likely cause.
